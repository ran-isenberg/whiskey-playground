# Lagavulin 16 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · 16 years · 43.0% ABV · $$$ · Rating 89/100

## Description

A benchmark Islay malt: deep, rich peat smoke wrapped around dried fruit and sherried sweetness. Powerful but rounded, like a long conversation by a fire.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Thick peat smoke, iodine, seaweed and dried fruit.
- **Palate:** Rich and oily: smoke, toffee, sherry sweetness and sea salt.
- **Finish:** Very long, smoky and warming with a hint of oak.

## Story

Made on the southern shore of Islay since 1816, its long sixteen-year maturation tames the peat into something deep and velvety.

- **Moods:** contemplative, brooding, cozy, nostalgic
- **Occasions:** fireside evening, winter night, after dinner
- **Serve:** neat, splash of water
