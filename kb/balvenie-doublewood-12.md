# The Balvenie DoubleWood 12 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Speyside, Scotland · 12 years · 40.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 85/100

## Description

Matured in bourbon then sherry casks: honeyed, nutty and smooth with gentle spice.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Honey, vanilla and sherry sweetness.
- **Palate:** Nutty, cinnamon, honey and sherry.
- **Finish:** Long and warming.

## Story

Balvenie still grows some of its own barley, malts on its own floors and employs coopers and a coppersmith on site.

## Best For

- **Moods:** comforting, warm, relaxed, romantic
- **Occasions:** date night, Sunday afternoon, after dinner
- **Serve:** neat, on the rocks
