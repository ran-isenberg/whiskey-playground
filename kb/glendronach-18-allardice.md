# GlenDronach 18 Year Old Allardice

**Single Malt Scotch** · Highlands, Scotland · 18 years · 46.0% ABV · $$$$ · Rating 89/100

## Description

A decadent Oloroso sherry-matured Highlander: Christmas cake, dark chocolate, espresso and spice.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Plum, raisin, dark chocolate and leather.
- **Palate:** Rich, silky dried fruit and coffee.
- **Finish:** Long and warming.

## Story

Named after founder James Allardice, who opened the distillery in 1826.
