# Auchentoshan Three Wood

**Single Malt Scotch** · Lowlands, Scotland · No age statement · 43.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 81/100

## Description

Triple-distilled and matured in three cask types: sweet, smooth, with brown sugar, raisins and hazelnut.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Brown sugar, blackcurrant, hazelnut.
- **Palate:** Sweet sherry, raisins and toffee.
- **Finish:** Long and fruity.

## Story

Auchentoshan, near Glasgow, triple-distils every drop, which is unusual in Scotland.
