# The Glenlivet 12 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Speyside, Scotland · 12 years · 40.0% ABV · $ · Rating 80/100

## Description

Smooth, floral and fruity: pineapple, honey and vanilla. An easy-drinking Speyside classic.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Tropical fruit, floral, summer meadow.
- **Palate:** Pineapple, vanilla and honey.
- **Finish:** Long, creamy and smooth.

## Story

The Glenlivet was one of the first licensed distilleries in Scotland, in 1824.

- **Moods:** relaxed, easygoing, optimistic, calm
