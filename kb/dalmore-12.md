# The Dalmore 12 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Highlands, Scotland · 12 years · 40.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 81/100

## Description

Chocolate orange in a glass: rich sherry, citrus and spice with a smooth, indulgent texture.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Orange marmalade, chocolate and vanilla.
- **Palate:** Sherry, citrus and spice.
- **Finish:** Medium, chocolatey.

## Story

The stag emblem on every bottle dates back to 1263, when an ancestor of the Mackenzie clan saved King Alexander III from a stag.
