# Ardbeg Uigeadail

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · No age statement · 54.2% ABV · $$$ · Rating 90/100

## Description

Ardbeg's smoke married with sherry casks: dark, intense and rich, with raisins, Christmas cake and embers.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Christmas cake, campfire smoke, raisins and leather.
- **Palate:** Thick, sweet smoke, dark chocolate, dates and spice.
- **Finish:** Very long, warming and smoky.

## Story

Named after the loch that supplies the distillery's water, it is a perennial favourite among peat enthusiasts.

- **Moods:** brooding, celebratory, contemplative, intense
