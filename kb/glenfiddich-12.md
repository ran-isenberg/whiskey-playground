# Glenfiddich 12 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Speyside, Scotland · 12 years · 40.0% ABV · $ · Rating 80/100

## Description

The world's best-known single malt: fresh pear, light oak and a creamy, easy finish.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Fresh pear, apple and subtle oak.
- **Palate:** Sweet fruit, butterscotch and cream.
- **Finish:** Long, smooth and mellow.

## Story

Still family owned by William Grant & Sons, Glenfiddich helped pioneer single malt as a category in the 1960s.
