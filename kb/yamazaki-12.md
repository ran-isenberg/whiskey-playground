# Yamazaki 12 Year Old

**Single Malt Japanese** · Osaka, Japan · 12 years · 43.0% ABV · $$$$ · Rating 86/100

## Description

Japan's flagship malt: elegant peach, pineapple and honey with a touch of incense-like Mizunara oak.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Peach, pineapple, grapefruit and vanilla.
- **Palate:** Coconut, cranberry and spice.
- **Finish:** Long, sweet ginger and cinnamon.

## Story

Yamazaki, near Kyoto, was Japan's first malt whisky distillery, founded in 1923.
