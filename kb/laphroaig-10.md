# Laphroaig 10 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · 10 years · 43.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 86/100

## Description

Unapologetically medicinal and smoky: love it or hate it. Bold peat, iodine and sea spray with a surprising sweet core.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Hospital-grade iodine, tar, peat smoke and seaweed.
- **Palate:** Big smoke, salt, a touch of vanilla and liquorice.
- **Finish:** Long, dry, smoky and tarry.

## Story

The famously divisive Islay malt that carries a Royal Warrant, built on peat cut from its own moss beds.

## Best For

- **Moods:** bold, adventurous, defiant, energetic
- **Occasions:** bonfire, camping, first peat experience
- **Serve:** neat, on the rocks, smoky highball
