# Glenfarclas 15 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Speyside, Scotland · 15 years · 46.0% ABV · $$$ · Rating 87/100

## Description

A family-owned sherry bomb with depth: walnuts, raisins, marmalade and gentle spice.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Sherry, walnut, marmalade and toffee.
- **Palate:** Full, rich dried fruit, nuts and spice.
- **Finish:** Long, warming and sweet.

## Story

Glenfarclas has been owned and run by the Grant family since 1865, one of the last truly independent distilleries.
