# Caol Ila 12 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · 12 years · 43.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 85/100

## Description

An elegant, lighter-style Islay malt: clean smoke, citrus and a fresh sea breeze. Smoky but never heavy.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Fresh smoke, lemon, sea air and herbs.
- **Palate:** Oily, smoky, gently sweet with citrus.
- **Finish:** Long, peppery and smoky.

## Story

Its stillhouse windows look straight across the Sound of Islay to the Paps of Jura.
