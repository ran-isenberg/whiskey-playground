# Ardbeg Ten Years Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · 10 years · 46.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 87/100

## Description

Intensely peaty yet surprisingly bright, with lemon zest, black pepper and espresso under the smoke.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Peat smoke, lemon, lime, and tarry rope.
- **Palate:** Explosive smoke, citrus, chocolate and espresso.
- **Finish:** Long, smoky and peppery.

## Story

Ardbeg came close to vanishing in the 1980s and 90s before being revived to cult status by smoke-lovers worldwide.

- **Moods:** energetic, bold, adventurous, fresh
- **Occasions:** beach walk, stormy day, outdoor adventure
