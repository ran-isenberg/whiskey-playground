# Glengoyne 21 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Highlands, Scotland · 21 years · 43.0% ABV · $$$$ · Rating 87/100

## Description

Slow-distilled and unpeated, aged 21 years in sherry casks: toffee, baked apples and warm spice.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Sherry, baked apples, toffee and cinnamon.
- **Palate:** Rich, fruity and sweet.
- **Finish:** Long and spicy.

## Story

Glengoyne distils slower than almost anyone, and its barley is air-dried with no peat smoke at all.

- **Moods:** luxurious, celebratory, nostalgic, grateful
- **Occasions:** milestone birthday, retirement, special occasion
