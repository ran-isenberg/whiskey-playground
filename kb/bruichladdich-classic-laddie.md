# Bruichladdich The Classic Laddie

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · No age statement · 50.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 84/100

## Description

An unpeated Islay malt that breaks expectations: floral, fruity and lightly salty, made from 100% Scottish barley.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Wildflowers, citrus, barley and a sea breeze.
- **Palate:** Clean and malty with apricot, honey and salt.
- **Finish:** Medium, fresh and lightly salty.

## Story

Bruichladdich champions terroir and transparency, sourcing Scottish barley and bottling without colouring or chill filtration.
