# Laphroaig Quarter Cask

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · No age statement · 48.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 87/100

## Description

Laphroaig finished in small quarter casks for extra oak contact: smoke softened by sweet vanilla and coconut.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Peat smoke, coconut, banana and vanilla.
- **Palate:** Sweet smoke, spice, oak and sea salt.
- **Finish:** Long, warm and smoky with lingering sweetness.

## Story

Inspired by the small casks once carried by pack horses, which mature whisky faster through more wood contact.
