# Kilchoman Machir Bay

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · No age statement · 46.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 85/100

## Description

A young, vibrant farm-distillery peater: bright smoke, vanilla and citrus with a sweet sherry touch.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Peat smoke, vanilla, lemon and sea spray.
- **Palate:** Sweet, smoky and fruity, with tropical hints.
- **Finish:** Medium-long, smoky and sweet.

## Story

Kilchoman, founded in 2005, was Islay's first new distillery in over a century and grows some of its own barley.
