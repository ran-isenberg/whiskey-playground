# Bowmore 12 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islay, Scotland · 12 years · 40.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 83/100

## Description

A gentle, balanced Islay: soft smoke, honey and lemon, with a whisper of dark chocolate. Peat for beginners.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Light smoke, lemon, honey and heather.
- **Palate:** Smooth honey, gentle peat and dark chocolate.
- **Finish:** Medium, soft smoke.

## Story

Bowmore is one of Scotland's oldest distilleries (1779) and still runs its own traditional floor maltings.

- **Moods:** calm, balanced, relaxed, friendly
- **Occasions:** first peat experience, casual evening, with friends
