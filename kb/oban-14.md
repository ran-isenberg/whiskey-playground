# Oban 14 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Highlands, Scotland · 14 years · 43.0% ABV · $$$ · Rating 84/100

## Description

A bridge between Highland and Island: orange, honey, sea salt and a wisp of smoke.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Orange, sea salt, honey and smoke.
- **Palate:** Rich, fruity and smooth.
- **Finish:** Long, smooth, sweet and oaky.

## Story

One of Scotland's smallest distilleries, the town of Oban grew up around it after it was founded in 1794.
