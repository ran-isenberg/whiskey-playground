# Highland Park 12 Year Old Viking Honour

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islands, Scotland · 12 years · 40.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 84/100

## Description

From Orkney: heather honey and soft, aromatic peat smoke in perfect balance. An all-rounder.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Heather honey, gentle smoke.
- **Palate:** Sweet honey, soft smoke, citrus.
- **Finish:** Medium, smoky and dry.

## Story

Distilled on Orkney with peat full of heather, giving a floral, aromatic smoke unlike Islay's.

## Best For

- **Moods:** balanced, adventurous, calm, brave
- **Occasions:** hiking trip, cozy evening, first peat experience
- **Serve:** neat, on the rocks
