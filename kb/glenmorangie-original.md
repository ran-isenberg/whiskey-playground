# Glenmorangie The Original 10 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Highlands, Scotland · 10 years · 40.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 81/100

## Description

Delicate and elegant: orange, peach, vanilla and floral notes from some of Scotland's tallest stills.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Citrus, peach, vanilla.
- **Palate:** Creamy, sweet, floral and fruity.
- **Finish:** Clean and medium.

## Story

Glenmorangie's stills are about the height of a giraffe, producing a very light, refined spirit.
