# The Balvenie Caribbean Cask 14 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Speyside, Scotland · 14 years · 43.0% ABV · $$$ · Rating 85/100

## Description

Finished in casks that held Caribbean rum: toffee, tropical fruit and vanilla. A holiday in a glass.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Toffee, vanilla, tropical fruit.
- **Palate:** Sweet, creamy, fruity with a hint of rum.
- **Finish:** Lingering and soft.

## Story

Malt master David Stewart pioneered cask finishing, and this rum-finished expression is one of his playful experiments.
