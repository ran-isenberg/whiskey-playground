# The Macallan 12 Year Old Sherry Oak

**Single Malt Scotch** · Speyside, Scotland · 12 years · 43.0% ABV · $$$ · Rating 85/100

## Description

Classic sherry-cask Speyside: dried fruit, ginger, wood spice and a rich, rounded mouthfeel.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Dried fruits, ginger, vanilla and sherry.
- **Palate:** Rich dried fruit, wood smoke and spice.
- **Finish:** Medium-long, sweet toffee and dried fruit.

## Story

Matured exclusively in sherry-seasoned oak casks from Jerez, The Macallan is among the most famous names in whisky.
