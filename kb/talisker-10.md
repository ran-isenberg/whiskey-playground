# Talisker 10 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Islands, Scotland · 10 years · 45.8% ABV · $$ · Rating 86/100

## Description

Born of the sea on Skye: peppery, smoky and briny, with a hot chili-pepper finish.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Sea salt, smoke, citrus and pepper.
- **Palate:** Dried fruit, smoke, peppery heat.
- **Finish:** Long, warming, peppery.

## Story

The oldest working distillery on the Isle of Skye, perched on the shores of Loch Harport.
