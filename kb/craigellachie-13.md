# Craigellachie 13 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Speyside, Scotland · 13 years · 46.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 84/100

## Description

A bold, old-fashioned Speysider: waxy, slightly sulphurous and fruity, nothing like the 'light Speyside' cliché.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Pineapple, struck match, beeswax.
- **Palate:** Full, oily, fruity and meaty.
- **Finish:** Medium, dry and nutty.

## Story

Uses traditional worm-tub condensers, which keep a heavier, old-school spirit character.

## Best For

- **Moods:** curious, rebellious, adventurous
- **Occasions:** something different, whisky nerd night
- **Serve:** neat
