# Clynelish 14 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Highlands, Scotland · 14 years · 46.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 86/100

## Description

Famously waxy and coastal: honey, orange, candle wax and a salty breeze. A bartender's favourite.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Beeswax, honey, orange and sea air.
- **Palate:** Oily, honeyed, slightly salty.
- **Finish:** Long, dry and spicy.

## Story

From the northern Highlands village of Brora, Clynelish is a key malt in Johnnie Walker blends.

- **Moods:** calm, curious, contemplative, fresh
- **Occasions:** coastal walk, quiet evening, reading a book
