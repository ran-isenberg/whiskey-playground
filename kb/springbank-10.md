# Springbank 10 Year Old

**Single Malt Scotch** · Campbeltown, Scotland · 10 years · 46.0% ABV · $$$ · Rating 88/100

## Description

A cult favourite: complex, oily and coastal, with pear, vanilla, brine and a whisper of smoke.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Pear, sea salt, vanilla and peat.
- **Palate:** Oily, fruity, malty and salty.
- **Finish:** Long and lingering.

## Story

Springbank does everything on site, from floor malting to bottling, the only Scottish distillery to do so.
