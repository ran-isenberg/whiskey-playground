# Green Spot

**Single Pot Still Irish** · Cork, Ireland · No age statement · 40.0% ABV · $$ · Rating 85/100

## Description

A fresh, apple-and-honey pot still Irish with a peppery spice. Bright and cheerful.

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose:** Green apple, pepper, barley.
- **Palate:** Spicy, sweet and fresh.
- **Finish:** Medium, toasted oak.

## Story

Originally bottled for the Mitchell & Son wine merchants of Dublin, who marked their casks with spots of paint.
